# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the Contributor Covenant.

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Assume good intent and ask questions before you criticize.
- Keep feedback specific and useful.
- No harassment, discrimination or personal attacks.

Maintainers may remove comments, issues or contributions that break these rules.
To report a problem, open a private security advisory or contact the maintainer through GitHub.
